About us

A small studio that treats your yard like its own

Fern & Stone started with one truck, a wheelbarrow, and the belief that a well-made garden should outlive the people who planted it.

Today we’re still a tight, local crew. The person who sketches your garden is on site when it goes in the ground, and the same hands come back each season to keep it thriving. No subcontractor roulette — just steady, honest work.

  • Licensed & insuredFully covered crews and clean job sites, every time.
  • Native plants firstLandscapes that fit the climate and feed the pollinators.
  • Clear, fixed quotesYou approve the number before a single shovel hits dirt.
  • Work we stand behindPlants and hardscape guaranteed — if it fails, we make it right.
